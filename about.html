About HoneyLight Creations

A small business built on big love for books and creativity.

Our Story

HoneyLight Creations was born from a love of books and the belief that every reader deserves to wear their passion. We create custom shirts, stickers, and prints for the book lovers, the audiobook addicts, the "anti-social book club" members, and everyone in between.

Every item is made to order — you pick the design you love, and we bring it to life just for you. We believe in supporting the reader community and making high-quality, affordable pieces that spark joy and conversation.

Thank you for supporting a small business. It means the world. 💗

Custom Orders

Don't see exactly what you're looking for? We love custom orders! Reach out and tell us your vision — we'll do our best to make it happen.

We also accept future orders on designs that aren't currently in stock. Just let us know and we'll add it to the queue.
